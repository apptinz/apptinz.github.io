Privacy Policy

Clean Master · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Clean Master app ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

  • Your photos, videos and contacts are processed on your device. We do not upload them to our servers and we cannot see them.
  • Items in the Private Photos vault are stored locally on your device in the App's storage.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We use Firebase to understand how the App is used and to deliver configuration. Purchases are handled by Apple.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following services, which may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) – collects app usage events and device/app information such as device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate region and an app-instance identifier, to help us improve the App. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google) – delivers app configuration and feature settings to your device.
  • Apple iCloud Drive – used only if you tap Backup for contacts. The backup file is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit – processes purchases and subscriptions. We validate purchase receipts with Apple to unlock premium features. We do not receive or store your payment card details or Apple ID credentials.

The App also checks a small configuration file hosted on our website. Requesting it may expose your IP address to the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) as with any web request.

4. Tracking

If the App displays Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, you may choose Ask App Not to Track. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The App does not use your photos, videos or contacts for advertising or tracking.

5. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App's cleaning, contact management and private vault features.
  • To verify purchases and manage premium access.
  • To understand usage, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To respond to your support requests.

6. Data retention and your control

Data processed on your device stays on your device and is removed when you delete it or uninstall the App. Analytics data held by Firebase is retained according to Google's settings. If you email us for support we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to help you.

Because we do not hold your photos, videos or contacts, we cannot access, export or delete them for you. You can manage them directly in the App, the Photos app, the Contacts app and iOS Settings. To ask about any data we may hold about you, contact us at the email below.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its processing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise your rights, email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We rely on iOS sandboxing and on-device processing to protect your data, and the Private Photos vault is protected by a passcode and optional biometrics. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page and the "Last updated" date will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the new policy.
