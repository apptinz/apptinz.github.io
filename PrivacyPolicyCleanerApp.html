Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Clean Master app ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information stored on your device

The App needs access to your photos, videos and contacts to find duplicates, large files and similar items, and to help you back up, merge or delete them. This processing happens on your device. We do not upload your photos, videos or contacts to our servers.

2. Permissions

  • Photos & Videos: to scan and clean duplicate, similar and large media, and to store items in the private vault.
  • Contacts: to find duplicate contacts, merge, back up and export them.
  • Camera / Microphone: only when you choose to capture a photo or video inside the App.

You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Third-party services

The App may use third-party services that collect limited information such as device identifiers, usage and diagnostics data: Firebase (analytics and crash reporting), Google AdMob (advertising) and in-app purchase services. These services have their own privacy policies.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through the support link on the App's App Store page.
