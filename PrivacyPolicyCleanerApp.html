Privacy Policy

CleanUp Phone - Cleaner Master · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the CleanUp Phone - Cleaner Master app ("the App", "we", "us") accesses and collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Your photos, videos and contacts are processed on your device. We do not upload them to our servers and we cannot see them.
  • The App is free to download with in-app purchases and contains advertising. Our analytics and advertising partners may collect identifiers, usage data and diagnostics, and may use them to show ads and measure performance.
  • You can decline tracking at any time (see Section 5).
  • We do not sell your photos, videos or contacts.

4. Third-party services

The App uses the following types of services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics and Remote Config (Google) – app analytics, diagnostics and delivery of app configuration. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Third-party advertising partners – to show and measure ads. They may collect identifiers, usage data, advertising data and coarse location, and may combine it with data from other apps and sites to deliver personalized ads, only where permitted by your tracking choice.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit – processes purchases and subscriptions. We validate purchase receipts with Apple to unlock premium features. We do not receive or store your payment card details or Apple ID credentials.
  • Apple iCloud Drive – used only if you tap Backup for contacts. The file is stored in your personal iCloud account and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The App also downloads a small configuration file from our website, which may expose your IP address to our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) like any web request.

5. Tracking and your ad choices

We and our advertising partners may use identifiers, purchase information, usage data and diagnostics to track activity across apps and websites owned by other companies for advertising and measurement. When required, the App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

  • Choose Ask App Not to Track, or change it at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Limit ad personalization on your device in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using your advertising identifier.

The App never uses your photos, videos or contacts for advertising or tracking.

6. How we use information

  • To provide the cleaning, contact management and private vault features.
  • To verify purchases and manage premium access.
  • To show advertising and measure its performance.
  • To understand usage, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To respond to your support requests.

7. Sharing

We share the data described in Sections 3 and 4 with analytics, advertising and infrastructure providers that help us operate the App, and when required by law or to protect our rights. We do not sell your photos, videos or contacts. Where privacy laws treat sharing identifiers for advertising as "sharing" or "sale" of personal information, you can opt out as described in Sections 5 and 9.

8. Retention

Data processed on your device stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Analytics and advertising data held by third parties is retained under their policies. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California and other US states under CCPA/CPRA and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, to opt out of targeted advertising or sharing, and to withdraw consent. We do not hold your photos, videos or contacts, so we cannot access or delete them for you; you manage them directly on your device. To exercise your rights, email us. We will not discriminate against you for doing so.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We rely on iOS sandboxing and on-device processing, and the private vault is protected by a PIN or pattern and optional biometrics. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page and the "Last updated" date will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the new policy.
