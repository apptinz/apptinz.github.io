Need help, found a bug or have a suggestion? Email us at tinhntk.dev@gmail.com. To help us respond faster, include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description (screenshots help). We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to delete duplicate or similar photos?

The App only deletes items you select. Review each group before confirming. Deleted photos move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them for about 30 days.

The App can't see my photos or contacts

Open iOS Settings > Clean Master, then set Photos to Full Access and turn on Contacts. If you chose "Limited" access, the App only sees the photos you selected.

How do I back up or export my contacts?

Open the Contacts section in the App, choose Backup or Export, and pick where to save or share the file. Backup saves a .vcf file to your iCloud Drive, so iCloud Drive must be turned on in iOS Settings. Export lets you share the file wherever you choose. Backups are created only when you ask for them.

I forgot my Private Photos passcode

If Face ID / Touch ID is enabled you can use it to unlock. Because the vault is stored only on your device and we cannot access it, we cannot recover a forgotten passcode. Deleting the App will permanently remove vault contents, so try to unlock first.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the subscription screen in the App and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Clean Master and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the App does not cancel it. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com, as Apple handles all billing.

Does the App upload my photos or contacts?

No. Scanning and cleaning happen on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The App is slow or a scan seems stuck

Large libraries can take a few minutes to scan. Keep the App open, make sure your device has some free storage, and try force-quitting and reopening it. If it still happens, email us with your photo count and iOS version.
